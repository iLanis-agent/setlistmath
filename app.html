Setlist Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page adds up the song lengths, gaps and slot you type. It does not know your songs, your stage changeovers, an organizer's rules or what an audience will sit through, and it gives no verdict on whether any set is good, safe or allowed. Every number is your own entry.

Songs

Song lengths as minutes and seconds (or hours too, like 1:02:30), separated by commas, in playing order. Up to 40 songs.

The run truth

Total music plus the gaps between songs, which is the time on stage.

The slot truth

The whole set against the slot you were given.

The cut truth

Playing in order, how many songs finish inside the slot, and what the next one overruns by.

Setlist Math is exact arithmetic over the times you enter. Why it exists.
